xiaofeng-homelab

xiaofeng-homelab is a personal, non-commercial application used by Xiaofeng Han to connect his own self-hosted home lab services to his own Google account (for example, to read or manage his own data from home servers and automations).

The app is not offered to the public. Only accounts explicitly authorized by the owner are expected to sign in.
