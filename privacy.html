Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how xiaofeng-homelab ("the app"), a personal home lab application operated by Xiaofeng Han, handles information obtained through Google sign-in and Google APIs.

Who uses the app

The app is for the personal use of its owner and members of his household. It is not offered to the general public.

Information accessed

The app only accesses the Google account information and data covered by the permissions (scopes) that you explicitly grant on Google's consent screen, such as your name, email address, and any account data needed for the home lab feature you authorized.

How information is used

Data is used solely to provide the home lab functions you requested. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to those functions.

Storage and security

Any data and access tokens are stored only on servers owned and operated by the app owner on a private home network, protected by reasonable security measures. Data is not stored with third-party services except where required to provide the requested function.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google APIs is never sold, and is not shared with or transferred to third parties, except as required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the function you authorized. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can request deletion of any stored data by emailing the contact below.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above will reflect the latest version.
